GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Need details about a particular class or subject? Send an enquiry and confirm current availability.

Which classes does Jnanapith Academy support?+

The academy is planned for Classes VI–XII. Specific batches and subjects depend on the timetable and confirmed teaching team.

Do you support WBBSE and CBSE?+

The intended programme covers WBBSE and CBSE. Please mention the student's class, board and school medium when enquiring.

Are classes available in Bengali and English?+

Bengali and English learning support is planned. The appropriate teaching language and materials will be discussed during enquiry.

How can I check fees and batch timings?+

Fees, available subjects, batch timings and admission details should be confirmed directly with the academy before enrolment.

Will online classes be available across West Bengal?+

Online expansion is part of the long-term plan. Contact the academy to confirm whether an online batch is currently available for your class and subject.
